About This Dashboard

This dashboard aggregates Iran conflict coverage from multiple credible international news sources. Al Jazeera serves as the primary source, supplemented by BBC News, Reuters, The Guardian, France 24, and Fars News (English). Content refreshes automatically every 2 minutes.
